# Product

Status: the approved Penpot redesign is implemented in the website and verified locally. The design source contains 55 new boards, 49 native component assets, light and night themes, and named prototype flows. The app uses cool periwinkle plates, dark navigation, amber controls, and generous spacing. See docs/design/retro-field-guide.md for implementation checks.

## Register

product

## Users

Pokémon fans browsing on phones and desktop computers, looking up a familiar species or discovering Pokémon within a generation. Audience assumptions will be refined with the user.

## Product Purpose

An anonymous reference Pokédex. Help people find a Pokémon quickly, recognize it through artwork, and understand its forms, types, stats, evolution, abilities, and moves. Keep canonical species identity distinct from variants and shiny appearance.

## Brand Personality

Clear, curious, characterful. User-selected direction: a modern field guide with expressive Pokémon artwork and a clean modern retro feel. The latest visual reference supplies a periwinkle hardware chassis, crisp bevels, carbon navigation, and amber controls. Generous space between elements and transitions between states are explicit preferences. See docs/design/retro-field-guide.md for the current design contract.

## Anti-references

The user rejects the current overall UI/UX and requests a redesign from scratch. Do not carry forward its Apple-inspired glass treatment, layout, or motion as mandatory constraints. Avoid decorative dashboard metrics, controls competing with content, and long entrance animations that delay browsing.

## Design Principles

- Start with the user's browsing task, then reveal detail progressively.
- Let Pokémon identity and artwork create character.
- Make generation scope, search, and active filters easy to understand.
- Keep orientation when opening a species and returning to results.
- Treat mobile as a distinct composition, not a scaled desktop screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implemented baseline: visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, labeled controls, generous touch targets, and reduced-motion support. Do not convey types or selection through color alone.

## Redesign Process


Penpot is the design review surface. The user approved implementation of its screens and prototype examples on 2026-10-14. DESIGN.md and docs/design/retro-field-guide.md record the implemented direction. The Dribbble reference was blocked by human verification during the first pass; its visual details were not inspected.
